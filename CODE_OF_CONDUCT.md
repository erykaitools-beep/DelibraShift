# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration space.
Participants should be constructive, assume good faith, welcome evidence and
correction, and focus criticism on ideas, code, and reproducible results.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and abusive
or deliberately disruptive behavior are unacceptable. Maintainers may edit,
remove, or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.

Sensitive reports should use GitHub's private security-advisory channel.
Good-faith reports will be reviewed fairly and with appropriate privacy.
